About Me

Hello! My name is Karokh Jalil, and I am an undergraduate Software Engineering student currently completing my first year (UG1) at the University of Kurdistan Hewlêr (UKH). My passion for software engineering centers around learning how code compiles, how systems handle information behind the scenes, and how to convert pure logic into active interfaces.

Right now, my core objective is to master the fundamentals of programming. I enjoy building clean, structural layouts, debugging problems step-by-step, and understanding the core mechanics of how programming languages organize data.

I view my first year as the crucial building block for my entire engineering career. I am dedicated to writing readable code, understanding mathematical problem-solving concepts, and scaling my technical skillset every single day.

Software Engineering at UKH

The Software Engineering program at the University of Kurdistan Hewlêr (UKH) gives students a comprehensive introduction to computer science foundations from day one. The academic structure combines standard engineering methodologies with direct, hands-on lab training.

As first-year students, our curriculum centers deeply on mastering fundamental programming structures, learning algorithmic thought processes, exploring computer architectures, and discovering how computing systems communicate across networks.

The rigorous coursework pushes us to solve complex analytical problems and build coding habits that mirror modern software industry standards.

Why I Chose UKH

Selecting the right academic environment to build my engineering career was vital, and UKH was my primary choice. The university's completely English-taught platform ensures that our foundational learning uses global industry terms, preparing us directly for international technical fields.

UKH is well-regarded for its merit-based learning philosophy and high academic standards. It provides an encouraging, high-tech environment filled with updated computer laboratories and collaborative spaces where peers challenge each other to excel.

By studying software engineering here, I am constructing a solid technical foundation, gaining valuable guidance from dedicated instructors, and positioning myself optimally within a professional ecosystem.
